About

I am a Ph.D. Scholar (Integrated PG + Ph.D.) at the Department of Architecture & Planning, Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee. My interests are urban and rural planning, land-use planning, and transport planning.

I'm open to collaborations in urban analytics, remote sensing, and user-generated big data for land-use studies. Get in touch.
